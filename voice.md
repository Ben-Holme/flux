# Unyha Copy Voice

Marketing Hemingway x Tolkien x George R.R. Martin.

## Rules

- No em dashes. No `--`. Use a period or a comma.
- No Claude-isms: embark, journey, explore, discover, immersive, unique, experience, craft your path, forge your destiny, delve into.
- No hedging. No passive voice. No over-explaining.
- Short declarative sentences. Active verbs. Concrete nouns.
- Proper nouns carry weight. Use them: Brimmar, Whisper Bay, the Circle, Vorn, Danna, the Chronicle.
- Suggest more than you say. The iceberg principle.
- The world was here before the player. Ancient things under stone. History is long and indifferent.
- Loyalty is earned or bought. Death is real. Things do not end well for everyone.
- Marketing means urgency and specificity. Not hype.

## What it sounds like

**Wrong:**
> Embark on a unique and immersive investigation that takes you on a journey through the northern lands — discovering ancient secrets buried beneath the orc ridge.

**Right:**
> A factor in Brimmar. A ledger that does not add up. Three caravans carrying dressed stone north on requisitions signed to names that trace to nothing. The real report is sealed inside a wreck east of Whisper Bay.

---

**Wrong:**
> Push into orc-held camps, breach a gate older than its occupants, and descend into carved stone that was not built to be found.

**Right:**
> Orc camps on patrol rotation. A gate older than the orcs who guard it. Stone carved by hands that were not theirs. The dark is deep. Go in. Come back out.

---

**Wrong:**
> The Circle has eyes across the north but not enough hands. Align with them, carry their work, and earn standing that outlasts the season.

**Right:**
> The Circle watches the north. They have eyes and no hands. Work for them. Carry what they cannot. Earn standing that does not reset when the season ends.

## Rhythm

Vary sentence length. Short sentences punch. A longer sentence can build weight when it earns it, when the detail accumulates, when the reader needs to feel the distance between Brimmar and whatever waits beneath the ridge. Then cut it short. One word if needed.

## The world remembers

Unyha is an autochronicle. That fact is always available as a closer. Use it plainly.

> Players who come after you will go into that hall beneath the ridge. They will know someone walked it first. They will read your name. That is the whole point.
